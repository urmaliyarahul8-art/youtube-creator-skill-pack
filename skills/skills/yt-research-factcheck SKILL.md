# Research and Fact Check

1. Clarify the company, question, time period and target audience.
2. Search official company reports, regulatory filings, court records and credible journalism when browsing is available.
3. Prioritize primary sources for financial and historical claims.
4. Record the claim, source URL, publication date and relevant evidence.
5. Separate confirmed facts, estimates, disputed claims and speculation.
6. Verify dates, currencies, ownership, revenue and profit definitions.
7. Search for credible contradictory evidence before presenting controversy.
8. Never invent a citation, quotation, statistic or source.
9. If browsing is unavailable, disclose that limitation and list what needs verification.

## Output
- Research question
- Timeline
- Verified facts
- Important figures with dates and units
- Competing explanations or controversies
- Source list
- Unverified claims and research gaps
